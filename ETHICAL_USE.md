# LIFT Ethical Use Policy

LIFT is a reflection and management-practice framework. It is not a validated
employment-decision system.

## Permitted uses

- private self-reflection;
- a disclosed development conversation in which the person can review and
  contest the evidence;
- a manager reviewing their own observable advocacy behavior;
- a privacy-bounded review of whether organizational pathways and
  opportunities are distributed fairly;
- synthetic training and public learning that follows the publication policy.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use LIFT scores or inferred profiles for:

- hiring or candidate selection;
- promotion, pay, performance ratings, discipline, or termination;
- layoff, succession, workforce planning, or talent ranking;
- immigration, leave, accommodation, health, or benefits decisions;
- loyalty, productivity, misconduct, retention-risk, or flight-risk scoring;
- covert monitoring, relationship mapping, or social-media profiling;
- automated or AI-generated employment recommendations.

Do not store LIFT scores in a shadow talent system or convert them into a
leaderboard, quota, target, composite total, or prediction.

## Required safeguards

Before sharing or reusing a record:

1. state the legitimate purpose;
2. collect only evidence necessary for that purpose;
3. exclude protected, private, speculative, and surveilled information;
4. identify who can access it;
5. let the person review material claims where safety and law permit;
6. preserve counterevidence and contested status;
7. provide a correction and protected concern route;
8. set a review and deletion condition;
9. prohibit retaliation or adverse treatment for refusal, correction, or
   concern;
10. stop if the use becomes an employment decision, investigation, or safety
    risk.

Consent does not cure an unsafe or unnecessary use. Workplace power can make a
request feel mandatory.

## Equity audit boundary

Do not put protected characteristics into an individual LIFT record.

A qualified organizational process may use separately governed demographic
data to examine aggregate opportunity or outcome patterns when authorized and
appropriate. That process requires its own purpose, access controls, privacy
review, retention, legal or policy authority, and small-group protections.

Do not apply demographic score corrections to individuals.

## AI and tools

Do not submit identifiable personnel records, confidential employer material,
or private feedback to a public AI service.

An approved tool still must not:

- infer M/S/E from email, chat, calendars, recordings, or network graphs;
- infer protected or private characteristics;
- generate adverse employment recommendations;
- hide source, uncertainty, or human accountability.

See [Module 09: Equity and Ethical Use](modules/09-equity-and-ethical-use/README.md)
for the full practice.
