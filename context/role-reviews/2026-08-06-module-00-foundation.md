## Dissent and defers

- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** independent public research on sponsorship,
  managerial support, social capital, and visibility belongs in a later evidence
  comparison. Until then, weakest-link utility remains a practitioner
  hypothesis.
- **Practitioner Advocate defer:** Module 00 cannot become golden until an
  independent person uses the scoring record without author coaching.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must examine identity, remote
  work, disability, caregiving, contingent roles, frontline work, and unequal
  access before LIFT claims broad applicability.
