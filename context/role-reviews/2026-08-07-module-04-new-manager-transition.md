## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | The module connects management evidence to M/S/E without changing the core score rubric or duplicating advocacy and cascade modules. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Private reset, clock, behavior weights, and promotion effects could survive as public certainty. Closed by removing all numerical transition claims. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | A role change could trigger an automatic score reset or team experience multiplier. Closed by updating evidence rather than applying formulas. |
| Practitioner Advocate | pass | Four repeatable passes, two contracts, delegation record, and transition evidence record provide usable entry points. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | The module could blame a new manager for inherited constraints or make the team absorb unsupported transition costs. Closed with structural context and organization responsibilities. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Removed claims, practice hypotheses, open evidence needs, and Module 05 defers remain explicit. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | "Stop doing individual work" could devalue craft and create absentee management. Closed with player-coach cases, direct-work guardrails, and bounded specialist contribution. |
| People Manager | finding | The output refuge could become a stigmatizing identity label. Closed by defining a repeatable work pattern without diagnosing motive or suitability. |
| Senior Leader | finding | Delegation could be read as relinquishing accountability. Closed with explicit outcomes, constraints, check-ins, escalation, and manager accountability for conditions controlled. |
| People Partner | finding | Team experience could become a hidden promotion gate or expose identifiable feedback. Closed by prohibiting secret downward scores and requiring agreed privacy processes. |

## Assurance review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Goodhart Auditor | finding | Managers could optimize delegation count, feedback volume, meeting frequency, or survey scores. Closed by requiring usable conditions and counterevidence rather than activity volume. |
| Equity and Access Auditor | finding | New managers with fewer networks, less support, or inherited under-resourcing could be judged against advantaged peers. Closed by recording inherited context and organization-provided support. |
| Privacy and Publication Steward | pass | Examples are synthetic and contain no private employer, person, level, process, or source provenance. |
| Harm and Misuse Reviewer | finding | A failed transition could be used to stigmatize returning to individual contribution. Closed by making return a valid, supported role-fit decision. |

## Finding ledger

| Severity | Finding | Owner | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| major | Role transition could trigger an unsupported automatic score reset | maintainer | Scores change only when the relevance or content of evidence changes |
| major | The output-refuge concept could stigmatize specialist work | maintainer | Direct work is permitted with purpose, duration, ownership, and handoff guardrails |
| major | Team outcomes could be attributed entirely to the manager | maintainer | Inherited, structural, and individual conditions remain explicit |
| major | Team experience could become a hidden manager promotion score | maintainer | Secret downward scoring and formulaic survey use are prohibited |
| major | Delegation could become abandonment | maintainer | Delegation contract requires authority, context, support, and escalation |
| major | New managers could be held responsible without authority or workload change | maintainer | Contract with the manager's manager and organization duties are required |
| major | Management could remain framed as superior advancement | maintainer | Choosing not to manage and returning to individual contribution are valid |
| major | Activity counts could replace evidence of management conditions | maintainer | Feedback, meetings, and delegation volume are rejected as automatic proxies |

## Dissent and defers

- **People Manager dissent:** the four passes may overlap in real work; they
  should remain lenses rather than stage gates.
- **Individual Contributor defer:** Module 05 must provide safe methods for
  gathering team experience without requiring public criticism of a manager.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** the output refuge remains a practitioner
  hypothesis until independent use demonstrates that it improves transition
  decisions.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

Module 04 is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point but remains `draft`.
Independent-human usability and broader external-evidence gates remain open.
