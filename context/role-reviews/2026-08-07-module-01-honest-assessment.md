## Dissent and defers

- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** the sponsor question remains a practitioner
  hypothesis until independent LIFT testing shows it improves clarity or
  calibration.
- **Individual Contributor dissent:** even carefully framed scoring may increase
  anxiety for some users. Usability testing must measure emotional and
  behavioral effects, not only task completion.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must test whether evidence
  classes disadvantage remote, frontline, contingent, disabled, caregiving,
  or underrepresented practitioners.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

Module 01 is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point but remains `draft`.
