## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** private self-reflection must not require an
  organizational governance form; controls scale with sharing and power.
- **People Partner dissent:** formal demographic and protected-data practices
  vary by jurisdiction and agreement; this module cannot prescribe a universal
  collection method.
- **Privacy and Publication Steward defer:** any future LIFT software needs a
  separate threat model, data-flow review, access log, deletion proof, and
  abuse-case assessment before implementation.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** independent-human review must test whether the
  policy actually stops plausible manager and HR misuse.
