Review scope: concrete advocacy behavior, credit attribution, opportunity
allocation, sponsorship capacity, consent, fair access, and honest advocacy
after a miss.

This is synthetic design review, not independent-human validation.

## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | See-Name-Connect-Open-Back-Transfer links manager behavior to M/S/E evidence pathways without turning the module into generic praise advice. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Private generated correlations could survive as a hidden behavior ranking. Closed by removing rankings, thresholds, probabilities, and score effects. |
| Scoring Steward | pass | The module produces observable evidence for later scoring but does not assign secret employee scores. |
| Practitioner Advocate | pass | The sequence, advocacy floor, opportunity review, and manager self-review are usable without software or a large process. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | Sponsorship could reproduce favoritism, homophily, and unequal access. Closed with criteria, distribution review, consent, an advocacy floor, and additional sponsorship paths. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Removed rules and open questions remain explicit in the claim and learning ledgers. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | Visibility or stretch work could be imposed and then interpreted as ambition. Closed with informed consent, alternatives, and no-retaliation opt-out language. |
| People Manager | finding | Finite advocacy capacity is real, but no universal portfolio size is defensible. Closed by treating capacity as a design constraint without a numeric limit. |
| Senior Leader | pass | Context relay is accurate, bounded, and connected to decisions rather than indiscriminate name promotion. |
| People Partner | finding | Opportunity tracking could become surveillance or a shadow talent score. Closed by reviewing distribution and rationale without secret employee ratings or private-data publication. |

## Assurance review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Goodhart Auditor | finding | Managers could maximize mentions or visible assignments rather than management quality. Closed by rejecting dose-response claims and evaluating accuracy, fairness, support, and transferred access. |
| Equity and Access Auditor | finding | Ambiguous group work and informal assignments can reproduce unequal recognition. Closed through specific shared attribution, criteria, distribution review, and public evidence limits. |
| Privacy and Publication Steward | pass | All examples are synthetic and contain no private employer, person, level, process, or source provenance. |
| Harm and Misuse Reviewer | finding | "Backing" could become shielding poor performance or attacking decision-makers. Closed by requiring accurate claims, accountability, and legitimate scrutiny. |

## Finding ledger

| Severity | Finding | Owner | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| major | Synthetic advocacy rankings could be presented as empirical law | maintainer | Replaced with an unranked diagnostic sequence |
| major | A fixed three-person sponsorship limit could legitimize favoritism | maintainer | Removed universal limit; added advocacy floor and capacity-design responses |
| major | Stretch assignments could become uncompensated exposure or unwanted visibility | maintainer | Added consent, workload, support, fit, and opt-out checks |
| major | Attribution could create a hero narrative that erases collaborators | maintainer | Shared contribution and role-specific credit are required |
| major | Opportunity review could become a shadow talent system | maintainer | Distribution review is separated from employee scoring and private publication |
| major | Sponsorship may reproduce similarity bias and unequal networks | maintainer | Criteria, repeated-distribution review, broader sponsors, and transferred access are explicit |
| major | Advocacy after failure could conceal performance evidence | maintainer | Miss, ownership, system conditions, learning, and bounded next opportunity remain visible |
| major | Formal documentation could be wrongly dismissed | maintainer | Formal and informal advocacy are complementary with different purposes |

## Dissent and defers

- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** the six-action sequence remains a LIFT practice
  until independent use shows that it improves manager decisions.
- **People Manager dissent:** distribution review must stay lightweight; a
  burdensome process would reduce actual coaching and advocacy time.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must define a privacy-safe
  equity review and test outcomes across role, identity, location, and access.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

Module 02 is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point but remains `draft`.
Independent-human usability and broader external-evidence gates remain open.
