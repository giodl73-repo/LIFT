## Disposition

No critical or major actionable synthetic design finding remains.

The practices layer is accepted as a draft. Independent-human review should
test:

1. whether evidence-burden guidance reduces rather than adds work;
2. whether contribution functions improve attribution without becoming a
   checklist;
3. whether people can contest an ordinary record without unsafe escalation;
4. whether the casebook makes the full LIFT journey usable;
5. whether the practices transfer outside office and salaried knowledge work.
