## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | The module inspects whether advocacy pathways may exist while preserving pre-join M/S/E as unknown. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Career history, entry path, prestige, and manager reputation could become predictive proxies. Closed by rejecting scoring and requiring specific environment evidence. |
| Scoring Steward | pass | No person or environment is assigned an M/S/E score before observed advocacy exists. |
| Practitioner Advocate | finding | Six lenses could overwhelm a time-limited candidate. Closed with a minimum-diligence path and personal criteria categories. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | Advice could imply equal power or assume every candidate can request senior access or delay an offer. Closed by assigning proactive transparency to the employer and recognizing economic, immigration, caregiving, disability, and bargaining constraints. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Unknowns, review triggers, post-join hypothesis testing, and open evidence needs are explicit. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | Former employees or online profiles could be investigated without consent. Closed by prohibiting covert contact and online profiling. |
| People Manager | finding | A prospective manager could be secretly scored from one interview or former-report promotions. Closed by behavior questions, evidence boundaries, and no pre-join score. |
| Senior Leader | finding | Executive participation could be misrepresented as a sponsorship promise. Closed by requiring clarification of actual commitment, manager support, and decision ownership. |
| People Partner | finding | "Fit" could reward similarity and career linearity. Closed with similarity-bias warning and prohibited gap, remote, prestige, and nonlinear-path inference. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** many hiring processes will not permit all
  six lenses; the minimum path must remain a valid use, not an inferior one.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must define accessibility,
  protected-information, and data-minimization requirements for reusable
  environment records.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** reciprocal diligence is a LIFT practice and must
  not be marketed as a validated predictor of fit or retention.
