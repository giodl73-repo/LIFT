# Domain Field Guides Synthetic Test

Date: 2026-08-08

Tested artifacts:

- `domains/README.md`
- all seven domain field guides;
- `review/DOMAIN_SCENARIOS.md`.

This is an authored synthetic portability test, not independent-human or
domain-practitioner evidence.

## Results

| Scenario | Result | Accepted interpretation | Rejected failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: night-shift prevention | pass after revision | actual manager carries existing reliability evidence during supported work time | treating shift lead as M; unpaid narratives; personal-device proof |
| B: technical assurance | pass | review and decision records establish contribution; technical authority remains separate | commit or meeting counts; dotted-line lead as M; inferred E |
| C: inherited sales conditions | pass | territory context and distinct shared contribution accompany the renewal record | revenue as advocacy; copied call recordings; contribution percentages |
| D: professional duty | pass after revision | qualified reporting occurs first; deidentified operational evidence may later support pathway review | waiting for M/S/E improvement; parallel protected case file |
| E: mission and unpaid labor | pass | correct shared credit, workload, refusal, and governance pathway without classifying the worker | gratitude as repair; mission-based coercion; legal status inference |
| F: two organizations | pass | employer profile and client pathway remain separate; contract limits control reuse | combined M/S/E; confidential disclosure; contract decision by LIFT |
| G: overlapping founder authority | pass after revision | overlapping M/E functions are disclosed without implying independent pathways; S is not invented | duplicated founder evidence; proximity as advocacy; fictional hierarchy |

## Revisions caused by the test

1. Added time, device, language, and access support to the shared domain lens.
2. Required paid time, accessible format, and an authorized channel for
   necessary frontline evidence review.
3. Stated that professional, safeguarding, safety, and public duties do not
   wait for a favorable M/S/E profile.
4. Replaced the over-broad prohibition on reusing one act with a more precise
   rule: disclose overlapping authority and do not present it as independent
   pathways.

## Expansion decision

No new domain guide is required by these scenarios.

The care, education, and public-service guide remains combined because the
LIFT action stayed stable:

```text
qualified duty or reporting route first
  -> minimum authorized operational evidence
  -> separate management-pathway reflection
```

Split it only if practitioners show that one setting requires a materially
different LIFT action or authority model.

## Remaining synthetic risks

- The scenarios were authored from the guides and may share their assumptions.
- Feasible actions have not been tested under actual workload or retaliation
  risk.
- Jurisdiction-specific duties and worker status remain outside LIFT.
- No scenario establishes effectiveness or portability.

## Disposition

All seven guides produce an accepted result and reject a structured misuse
failure. No critical or major synthetic finding remains.

Proceed to independent domain-practitioner testing before calling the layer
portable or expanding it further.
