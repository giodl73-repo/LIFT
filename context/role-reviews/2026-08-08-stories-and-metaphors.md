## Second-pass changes

The fresh fixed-point review changed the draft:

- added a situation-based entry table and explicit intended audience;
- removed the implied claim that stories improve memory;
- exposed the held or draft lifecycle status of every FRAMES source;
- qualified chips-story inquiry for safety and power;
- changed contact mapping from unnecessary person-level relationships to roles
  or affected groups with a deletion event;
- changed transition categories from trust that "must be rebuilt" to trust
  evidence that requires current observation;
- removed an arbitrary three-option detail from the board illustration.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable synthetic design finding remains.

The companion page is accepted as a draft explanatory layer. Independent-human
review should test:

1. whether readers can state the direct rule after reading each story;
2. whether readers recognize when the chips story must stop;
3. whether readers distinguish contact pathways from private influence;
4. whether plain-language fallbacks work without the metaphor.
