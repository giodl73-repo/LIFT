# Module 08 Transitions Role Review

Date: 2026-08-07

Reviewed artifacts:

Review scope: manager succession, transfers, reorganizations, leave, external
moves, returning employees, handoffs, commitments, privacy, and evidence
continuity.

This is synthetic design review, not independent-human validation.

## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | The module keeps M/S/E attached to current advocacy functions while preserving historical evidence. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Transition language could reproduce unsupported reset and recovery claims. Closed by using evidence statuses and rejecting numeric baselines and clocks. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | A carried score could be mistaken for a current score. Closed by separating dated history, current status, and current evidence. |
| Practitioner Advocate | finding | Six statuses and a full record could be burdensome. Closed by one reusable loop and records that can be shortened to changed paths only. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | The source placed continuity work on the affected person. Closed by assigning handoff, interim authority, access, and commitment duties to the organization. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Review triggers, stale evidence, correction paths, and open evidence needs make revision explicit. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | A handoff could become a permanent private dossier or verdict. Closed by bounded fields, review of factual content, privacy exclusions, dates, and no personality inference. |
| People Manager | finding | Incoming managers could be required either to accept every old judgment or ignore it. Closed by treating the handoff as evidence that requires scope and current observation. |
| Senior Leader | finding | Reorganizations could leave decisions and commitments without an owner. Closed by explicit authority maps and commitment disposition. |
| People Partner | finding | Leave, transfer, and return could trigger adverse commitment or capability inference. Closed by paused evidence, reboarding, privacy, and evaluation of work performed. |

## Assurance review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Goodhart Auditor | finding | Transition speed, question counts, or early visibility could become a new optimization target. Closed by no fixed window, no required score velocity, and organizational evidence opportunities. |
| Equity and Access Auditor | finding | Informal handoffs could preserve evidence only for highly visible people. Closed by a role-consistent continuity process and an audit of lost evidence and commitments. |
| Privacy and Publication Steward | pass | Examples are synthetic and the handoff excludes private health, family, caregiving, protected, rumor, and surveillance information. |
| Harm and Misuse Reviewer | finding | Transition records could become succession, loyalty, or flight-risk lists. Closed by explicit prohibited uses and data minimization. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** small organizations may use only the
  changed-path status and commitment fields; full records must not become a
  compliance requirement.
- **People Manager dissent:** a serious unresolved concern may need restricted
  preservation even when the person cannot review the source; the stop
  conditions route this to a fair formal process.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must define retention,
  accessibility, protected-information, and anti-retaliation requirements for
  records used across modules.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** the six statuses are a LIFT practice, not a
  validated predictor of transition outcomes.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

Module 08 is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point but remains `draft`.
Independent-human usability and broader external-evidence gates remain open.
