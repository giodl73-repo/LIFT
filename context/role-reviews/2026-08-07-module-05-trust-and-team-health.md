## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | M/S/E remains an advocacy profile while team health becomes a separate companion record. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Survey, attrition, silence, and team feedback could be treated as direct causal proof. Closed with source limits, alternative explanations, and uncertainty. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | Team experience could become a hidden second M score or multiplier. Closed by prohibiting arithmetic and maintaining three separate records. |
| Practitioner Advocate | finding | Three records plus five lenses could become burdensome. Closed with one signal record and permission to use only material lenses. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | Senior sponsorship could suppress team concerns, or anonymous feedback could deny the manager any response. Closed with protected inquiry and procedural fairness. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Open evidence needs, serious-concern routing, and defers are explicit. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | Skip-level or survey processes could expose respondents or force advocacy for the manager. Closed with privacy limits, no compelled praise, and anti-retaliation rules. |
| People Manager | finding | Rumor or one departure could become an unreviewable verdict. Closed with behavioral specificity, context, response rights, and multi-causal interpretation. |
| Senior Leader | finding | Strong delivery could be used to dismiss harmful team conditions. Closed by separating output from team experience and management acts. |
| People Partner | finding | Ordinary development concerns and serious misconduct could be mixed. Closed by explicit formal-process routing and independence requirements. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** the safest version requires capable
  organizational support that many small organizations do not have.
- **Privacy and Publication Steward defer:** Module 09 must define minimum
  privacy and data-governance requirements for any reusable team-health tool.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** the five team-health lenses remain a LIFT
  organizing practice, not a validated measurement model.
