Review scope: independence, coercion, privacy, review theater, accessibility,

This is synthetic design review, not independent-human validation of the review
protocol.

## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | The protocol preserves module ownership and reviews one bounded task. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | One attempt could be described as validation. Closed with task-scoped evidence states and explicit non-claims. |
| Scoring Steward | pass | Reviewers and modules are not scored, averaged, ranked, or compared. |
| Practitioner Advocate | finding | Review could become a large audit. Closed by one module, one task, one scenario, one report. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | Workplace authority could compel participation or profile disclosure. Closed with voluntary participation and coercion exclusions. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Findings, dissent, disposition, artifact changes, and golden-gate effects are recorded. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | A public issue could expose a private record. Closed by synthetic scenarios, repeated warnings, and no completed-record admission. |

| Senior Leader | finding | Positive reports could become testimonials. Closed by a findings index rather than endorsement language. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** the full report remains too long for some
  users; first independent attempts should identify removable fields.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** the protocol itself needs review in
  non-Markdown and non-GitHub formats.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** no evidence state changes until a real independent
  attempt is admitted.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

The protocol is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point. The independent
