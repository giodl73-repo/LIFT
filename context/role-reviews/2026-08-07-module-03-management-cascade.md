## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | finding | The cascade could create fatalism that one manager determines a career. Closed with alternative legitimate routes and explicit rejection of hard ceilings. |
| People Manager | finding | Boundary work could become an unlimited expectation layered onto management. Closed by naming overload, coordination cost, confidentiality, and selective redundancy. |
| Senior Leader | finding | Direct access could be read as unmanaged bypass. Closed by requiring work purpose, consent, decision clarity, and coordination. |
| People Partner | finding | Network mapping could become a shadow measure of influence or promotability. Closed by mapping channel movement rather than contact count or employee political activity. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** five channels by five failure modes may
  still feel large; independent testing should determine whether the record
  needs a shorter entry path.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must define a privacy-safe way
  to inspect pathway distribution across identity, location, role, and work
  arrangement.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** no causal or numerical claim about manager network
  effects should be promoted without independent evidence beyond adjacent team
  research.
