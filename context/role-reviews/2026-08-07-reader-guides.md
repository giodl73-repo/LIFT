## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | Modules remain authoritative; guides route by questions rather than adding a model. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Direct prose could imply proven career outcomes. Closed by internal source links and explicit non-claims. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | Situation guides could recreate named score patterns. Closed by no pattern labels, sums, gates, corrections, or predictions. |
| Practitioner Advocate | finding | Nine guides could create another curriculum. Closed by question routing and one smallest action per guide. |
| Power Dynamics Steward | finding | Direct conversation advice could ignore retaliation. Closed with safe-question, pause, protected-route, and no-forced-disclosure language. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Guides follow modules and require independent review before stronger status. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** nine guides may still be too many; reader
  testing should identify which can merge.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** accessible non-Markdown formats remain
  untested.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** no guide is independently usable until a real
  person completes a task without author or AI interpretation.
- **People Partner defer:** the serious-concern guide should receive qualified
  labor, legal, and people-practice review.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

The reader library is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point and remains
a draft pending independent-human review.
