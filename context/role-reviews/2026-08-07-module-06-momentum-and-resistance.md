## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | The module adds time without changing the M/S/E evidence rubric or turning the model into prediction. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | Physical-style momentum, resistance, and friction formulas could survive as research claims. Closed by removing all arithmetic, coefficients, and forecasts. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | Ordinal score differences could become velocity and be summed across dimensions. Closed by using evidence events and separate movement states. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** the distinction between friction and
  resistance may remain difficult without an independent observer.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** Module 09 must define how structural
  barriers and formal correction routes are represented without exposing
  protected personal information.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** movement states remain LIFT practices and must not
  be described as validated predictors.
