## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Finding or reason |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | pass | Templates compose the accepted module semantics without replacing them. |
| Evidence Skeptic | finding | A toolkit could imply that record keeping improves outcomes. Closed by making no outcome claim and requiring independent use. |
| Scoring Steward | finding | History and tooling could reintroduce sums, patterns, velocity, or targets. Closed by preserving evidence context and banning totals, gradients, and score goals. |

## Dissent and defers

- **Practitioner Advocate dissent:** seven public templates may still be too
  many; independent users should identify a smaller core.
- **Equity and Access Auditor defer:** independent review must test screen
  reader use, cognitive load, language clarity, and non-Markdown equivalents.
- **Privacy and Publication Steward defer:** any software proposal requires a
  separate threat model and cannot inherit approval from this module.
- **Evidence Skeptic defer:** no toolkit field should become stable until
  independent practitioners use it without author coaching.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable design finding remains.

Module 10 is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point but remains `draft`.
