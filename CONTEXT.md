# LIFT Context

## Mission

Help people inspect how support, context, credit, and advocacy travel through
an organization—and improve those pathways without reducing people to scores.

## Core model

```text
M = direct-manager advocacy
S = next-level or skip advocacy
E = executive or senior-sponsor advocacy
```

Each dimension uses a 1–10 evidence rubric. The profile is written `M·S·E`.
The weakest evidence path is a diagnostic constraint, not a deterministic
promotion gate.

## Current phase

The repository is documentation-first and being developed module by module.
Modules 00 and 01 define the M/S/E foundation and honest-assessment contract.
Both are synthetic role-reviewed drafts and candidate golden modules.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No employer-specific levels, policies, documents, examples, or provenance.
- No unsupported precision or synthetic findings presented as empirical fact.
- No score as a measure of performance, potential, loyalty, morality, or human
  worth.
- No guaranteed promotion, retention, hiring, or compensation predictions.
- No software until the documentation and portable records demonstrate a
  repeated unmet need.
