# LIFT Context

## Mission

Help people inspect how support, context, credit, and advocacy travel through
an organization—and improve those pathways without reducing people to scores.

## Core model

```text
M = direct-manager advocacy
S = next-level or skip advocacy
E = executive or senior-sponsor advocacy
```

Each dimension uses a 1–10 evidence rubric. The profile is written `M·S·E`.
The weakest evidence path is a diagnostic constraint, not a deterministic
promotion gate.

## Current phase

The repository is documentation-first and being developed module by module.
Modules 00 through 10 define the M/S/E foundation, honest-assessment contract,
advocacy practice, management-cascade model, new-manager transition,
trust-and-team-health model, longitudinal evidence movement, and reciprocal
environment diligence. Module 08 adds evidence continuity through manager,
role, team, organization, and leave transitions. Module 09 adds the
framework-wide equity, privacy, anti-retaliation, data-governance, AI, and
prohibited-use contract. Module 10 adds plain private-first operating templates
without software or centralized scoring. A question-led reader-guide layer
routes common situations back to these modules without adding pattern
classifiers, predictions, or career decisions. They are synthetic role-reviewed
drafts. Modules 00 through 10 are candidate golden modules. Pre-join M/S/E
remains unknown.

A stories-and-metaphors companion page provides four bounded illustrative
explanations without changing module authority, scoring, or evidence rules.

A cross-cutting practices layer addresses evidence burden, collective
contribution, correction, and end-to-end application without creating Module
11 or changing the M/S/E model.

A domain field-guide layer translates evidence sources, authority, pathway
actions, and safety boundaries across work settings without changing the core
model.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No employer-specific levels, policies, documents, examples, or provenance.
- No unsupported precision or synthetic findings presented as empirical fact.
- No score as a measure of performance, potential, loyalty, morality, or human
  worth.
- No guaranteed promotion, retention, hiring, or compensation predictions.
- No software until the documentation and portable records demonstrate a
  repeated unmet need.
