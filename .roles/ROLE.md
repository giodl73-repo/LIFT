## Review contract

Roles return `pass`, `finding`, or `defer`.

A finding records severity (`critical`, `major`, or `minor`), artifact,
observed problem, consequence, owner, and closure condition. A defer names the
future module, evidence, or decision that will resolve it.

A module reaches role-review fixed point when:

- no critical or major actionable finding remains;
- evidence and publication roles explicitly pass;
- every defer has a destination;
- dissent remains visible;
- parliament precedes stakeholder, assurance, and editorial review.

Synthetic roles are design-review lenses, not independent people, external
experts, empirical evidence, or organizational endorsement.

## Required review order

1. Framework Steward
2. Evidence Skeptic
3. Scoring Steward
4. Practitioner Advocate
5. Power Dynamics Steward
6. Learning Loop Steward
7. Relevant stakeholders
8. Assurance
9. Editorial

Independent-human review remains a separate golden-module gate.
