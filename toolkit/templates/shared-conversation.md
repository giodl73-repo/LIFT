# LIFT Shared Conversation

Create this separately from a private profile. Share only what is necessary for
the stated conversation.

```text
Permitted purpose:
Participants:
What I am sharing:
What I am not sharing:

Question:
Specific observable evidence:
Counterevidence or uncertainty:
Relevant structural context:
What I want to understand:
Requested next action:

Agreed commitment:
Owner:
Review trigger:
Correction route:
Who may retain this record:
Deletion or archive condition:
```

Before sharing:

- apply the ethical-use check;
- remove private notes and protected information;
- do not disclose confidential sources;
- do not imply that participation is required;
- do not let the record become an employment-decision input.
