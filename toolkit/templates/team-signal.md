# LIFT Team Signal

Use this only for a legitimate management or team-learning purpose.

```text
Purpose:
Signal:
Evidence source:
People and period represented:
Specific observation or theme:
Plausible explanations:
Corroborating evidence:
Counterevidence:
Inherited or structural context:
Privacy or retaliation risk:
Responsible owner:
Safe next action:
Stop or formal-route condition:
Recheck trigger:
Deletion or archive condition:
```

Do not:

- create a hidden team score;
- expose identifiable feedback;
- infer trust from silence;
- turn one signal into a manager verdict;
- use the record for employment decisions.
