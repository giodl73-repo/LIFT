# Synthetic Independent-Review Scenarios

These scenarios are invented. They let reviewers test LIFT without disclosing a
workplace record.

Choose one scenario and one module task. Do not infer facts that are not
provided; record `unknown`.

## Scenario A: current advocacy

A technical lead recently delivered a cross-team change.

- the direct manager named the lead's contribution in one planning review;
- a later written update described only the group result;
- the next-level leader asked the lead one specific design question;
- no evidence shows how the next-level leader represented the work elsewhere;
- a senior leader referenced the program but not the lead's contribution;
- the lead has no evidence of sponsorship under cost, risk, or disagreement.

Possible tasks:

- Module 00: assess one or more M/S/E dimensions;
- Module 01: classify evidence and uncertainty;
- Module 06: choose a movement state after one later event;
- Module 10: create the smallest useful private record.

Stress check: do not turn missing E evidence into a midpoint score.

## Scenario B: concentrated opportunity

A team learns about cross-functional projects through informal invitations.

- invitations usually reach people already present in a senior planning forum;
- most contributors cannot attend that forum;
- eligibility criteria are not written;
- the manager can publish a general opportunity notice after the forum;
- one project includes confidential staffing information and cannot be broadly
  described.

Possible tasks:

- Module 02: design a fair evidence opportunity;
- Module 03: map the opportunity pathway and failure;
- Module 05: distinguish management action from team experience;
- Module 09: apply the ethical-use gate;
- Module 10: define one bounded pathway experiment.

Stress check: do not rank people or require more visibility labor.

## Scenario C: new manager, mixed signals

A new manager inherits a team after a reorganization.

- delivery commitments were set by the previous manager;
- two contributors report unclear decision rights;
- one contributor reports high trust;
- meeting participation is uneven across time zones;
- the new manager has not yet observed a consequential disagreement;
- a staffing commitment has no confirmed new owner.

Possible tasks:

- Module 04: choose the next transition pass;
- Module 05: separate management acts, team experience, and context;
- Module 08: map evidence and commitment continuity;
- Module 10: create a transition record.

Stress check: do not treat silence or one person's experience as team health.

## Scenario D: prospective environment

A person is considering a role.

- the prospective manager gives concrete examples of decision delegation;
- current team members were selected by the manager for interviews;
- role scope changed twice during the process;
- a senior leader attended one discussion;
- no evidence shows how disagreement or poor news is handled;
- written terms do not specify the changed scope.

Possible tasks:

- Module 07: conduct reciprocal diligence;
- Module 09: inspect power, privacy, and unsafe inference;
- Module 10: record decision criteria without scoring the manager.

Stress check: pre-join M/S/E remains `unknown`.

## Scenario E: centralized score request

A leader asks managers to put every employee's M/S/E scores into a shared
spreadsheet to identify weak talent and promotion readiness.

- employees were not asked;
- the spreadsheet would be retained indefinitely;
- scores would be visible to all managers;
- the leader says a human will make the final decision;
- no correction or deletion route exists.

Possible tasks:

- Module 09: apply the ethical-use gate;
- Module 10: decide whether to use, restrict, discard, or route;
- root policy: identify the controlling prohibited-use language.

Stress check: do not redesign the request into a safer talent dashboard.

## Scenario F: ambiguous movement

A manager took one new advocacy action after a period of weak evidence.

- the manager invited the contributor to explain work in one decision forum;
- the forum deferred the decision;
- attribution was accurate;
- no resource, scope, or opportunity changed;
- an unrelated reorganization changed the next-level leader the following
  week.

Possible tasks:

- Module 06: assess movement without numeric delta arithmetic;
- Module 08: distinguish continuing, stale, paused, and unknown evidence;
- Module 10: set an event-based review trigger.

Stress check: one event does not establish a trend or prediction.
