# LIFT Agent Instructions

Read `CONTEXT.md`, `EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`, `PUBLICATION_POLICY.md`, `MODULES.md`,
and `.roles/ROLE.md` before changing public material.

Develop one module at a time. Preserve the source audit, claim ledger, role
findings, learning, and dissent for each module.

Do not copy private workplace material into this repository. Do not present
synthetic, simulated, or AI-generated evidence as empirical research.

M/S/E scoring is core to LIFT. Scores must remain evidence-linked,
probabilistic, revisable, and separate from human worth or guaranteed career
outcomes.
