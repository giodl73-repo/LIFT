# Get an Honest Read

Use this guide when reassurance, uncertainty, and incomplete access make it
hard to describe current advocacy.

## Start with one dimension

Do not begin by producing a full profile.

Choose one:

```text
M: What specific action shows that my manager represented my work accurately
   when I was not present?

S: What specific action shows that the next organizational level has an
   independent, accurate view of my contribution?

E: What senior decision, resource, scope, or protection demonstrates informed
   sponsorship rather than visibility alone?
```

If the question is not relevant to your scope, leave the dimension `unknown`.

## Record evidence before score

```text
Question:
Evidence window:
Observable evidence:
Counterevidence:
What was reported by another person:
What I inferred:
Structural or access context:
Unknowns:
```

Separate:

- what you observed directly;
- what a trusted source reported;
- what ambient language suggests;
- what you inferred.

Do not turn warmth, praise, title, meeting attendance, or one compliment into
advocacy.

## Ask safely

A disclosed development question can be:

> Can you give me a recent example of how my work was represented in a
> conversation I did not attend?

Useful follow-ups:

- What evidence was most persuasive?
- What counterevidence or concern was raised?
- What decision could you make?
- What required another person's authority?
- What should I understand differently?

Do not demand disclosure of confidential meetings or another person's private
comments.

If asking could create retaliation or safety risk, do not force a direct
conversation. Use a safer qualified route or keep the question private.

## Choose a judgment

After the evidence:

```text
Provisional score or range:
Confidence:
What would change the judgment:
Review trigger:
```

Use `unknown` when the evidence cannot distinguish a range.

Do not:

- apply a fixed bias correction;
- choose the midpoint because evidence is absent;
- sum M/S/E;
- infer private conviction;
- convert a score into promotion readiness.

## Common misreads

| Observation | Safer interpretation |
|---|---|
| "My manager says I am doing well" | supportive feedback; advocacy remains a separate question |
| "I presented to a senior leader" | access or visibility; sponsorship remains unknown |
| "My name appeared in a document" | attribution evidence; downstream action remains unknown |
| "Nothing negative happened" | absence of negative evidence, not positive advocacy |
| "I cannot observe the room" | an access limitation; record `unknown` rather than inventing certainty |

## Smallest next action

Choose one:

- ask for one specific example;
- request correction of one attribution error;
- identify who owns one decision;
- wait for one relevant event;
- record that the answer is currently unknowable.

Use the [private profile](../toolkit/templates/private-profile.md).

## What this guide cannot tell you

- what another person privately thinks;
- whether a promotion or opportunity will occur;
- whether supportive language is sincere;
- whether a formal employment concern exists;
- whether direct inquiry is safe in your context.
