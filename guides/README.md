# LIFT Reader Guides

These guides help readers enter LIFT through a current question.

The [modules](../MODULES.md) remain authoritative. Guides add no new scoring
rules, empirical findings, or outcome predictions.

## Guide contract

Every guide:

- starts from observable evidence;
- preserves counterevidence and `unknown`;
- separates structural conditions from personal blame;
- avoids score totals and predictions;
- does not make employment decisions;
- uses the smallest relevant toolkit record;
- names a condition for stopping or routing elsewhere.

## Not in this library

There are no:

- employer-level guides;
- score-pattern identities;
- promotion timelines;
- discipline rankings;
- candidate or manager classifiers;
- stay-or-leave recommendations;
- signal batteries;
- hiring guides;
- hidden personnel assessments.

Blank records are in the [Operating Toolkit](../toolkit/README.md).

## Evidence boundary

The guides are LIFT practices composed from Modules 00-10. They require
independent-human usability and safety review.
