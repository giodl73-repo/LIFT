# Decide Whether to Stay, Transfer, or Leave

LIFT cannot calculate the correct career decision.

It can help separate evidence, constraints, commitments, and unknowns before
you decide.

## Name the decision

```text
Decision:
Decision owner:
Earliest decision event:
Latest safe decision event:
Purpose:
People affected:
Private or protected constraints:
```

Use dates only when they come from your actual constraints or commitments.
There is no universal review-cycle threshold.

## Describe the current environment

For the current role:

- What is working?
- What is not working?
- Which evidence pathway is weak or unknown?
- Is the condition local, organizational, or unclear?
- What commitment remains open?
- What has changed over time?
- What is unsafe to test?

Do not reduce the environment to an M/S/E total.

## Describe each option

For `stay`, `transfer`, and `leave`, record:

```text
What becomes possible:
What is preserved:
What is lost:
What becomes unknown:
New dependencies:
Written terms:
Reversibility:
Safety or retaliation concern:
Evidence still needed:
```

Add other options when relevant:

- change scope;
- change working agreement;
- pause;
- seek advice;
- use a formal route;
- decline an offered change.

## Run the smallest useful test

Before an irreversible decision, a bounded test may help:

- clarify one decision right;
- request disposition of one commitment;
- inspect one prospective environment condition;
- try one fair pathway change;
- seek qualified advice;
- verify one written term.

Do not run a test that exposes you to retaliation, delays urgent protection, or
requires another person to disclose confidential information.

## Decide from criteria, not score movement

```text
Must be true:
Must not be true:
Constraint:
Acceptable unknown:
Unacceptable unknown:
Evidence obtained:
Counterevidence:
Decision:
Reason:
Review condition:
```

A low or declining LIFT judgment is information, not an instruction to leave.

A high judgment is not an instruction to stay.

## Common traps

- treating frustration as the only evidence;
- treating a score total as expected value;
- assuming an internal transfer preserves advocacy;
- assuming an external move resets a person;
- following one manager without inspecting the new environment;
- waiting because a supportive promise lacks a review trigger;
- staying because sunk costs are large;
- leaving because a formula says so.

## Route when needed

Use qualified legal, labor, financial, immigration, health, safety, or people
support when the decision depends on those domains.

LIFT is not a substitute.

Use the
[environment-diligence record](../toolkit/templates/environment-diligence.md),
[transition record](../toolkit/templates/transition.md), and
[ethical-use check](../toolkit/templates/ethical-use-check.md).

## What this guide cannot tell you

- the expected financial return of an option;
- whether an internal transfer will be approved;
- whether a new environment is safer;
- whether a manager's support will transfer;
- which option is correct for you.
