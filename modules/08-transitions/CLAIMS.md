## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the six evidence statuses;
- transition records across frontline, shift, contract, public-sector, and
  nonprofit work;
- examples where a handoff harmed rather than helped;
- fair handling of contested handoff evidence;
- accessible reboarding for different leave and transfer types;
- evidence about preserving commitments during reorganizations;
- transition support for people without senior access;
- criteria for when old evidence is stale rather than continuing;
- counterexamples where formal continuity records increased surveillance;
- low-burden transition records for small organizations.
