# Synthetic Example: Reorganization and an Orphaned Commitment

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A manager committed to placing a person in a development assignment. Before the
assignment starts, a reorganization moves the work and budget to another
leader.

## Commitment ledger

```text
Commitment: lead the next customer migration
Original owner: former manager
Authority at the time: assignment owner
New owner: receiving director
Disposition: unknown
Reason: work and budget moved
Review date: two weeks after authority transfer
```

The receiving director reviews the purpose, current capacity, and allocation
criteria. They may reconfirm, revise, or close the commitment with a reason.

The old promise is neither guaranteed nor silently erased.
