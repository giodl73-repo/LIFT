# Synthetic Example: Internal Transfer Across a Decision Boundary

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A person transfers to a different division of the same organization.

Their former next-level leader remains supportive but has no authority over the
new role.

## Mapping

```text
Prior M: retired for the new role
Prior S: historical support, not a continuing S route
Prior E: stale because the new division has a different senior decision chain
Current M: unknown
Current S: unknown
Current E: unknown
Portable evidence: completed cross-division work and named contribution
```

The receiving team provides role onboarding and a current authority map.

The transfer did not erase the person's record. Staying with the same employer
also did not preserve M/S/E automatically.
