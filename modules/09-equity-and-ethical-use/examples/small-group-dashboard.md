# Synthetic Example: Small-Group Dashboard Re-Identification

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A dashboard reports average access and advocacy by location, role, and gender.
One intersection contains two people, and colleagues can identify both.

Names are absent.

## Review

```text
Purpose: aggregate pathway review
Re-identification risk: high
Source protection risk: high
Action: suppress the cell and redesign the grouping
```

The organization retains the protected analysis only with the qualified audit
team and reports a broader pattern that cannot reasonably identify people.

Removing names did not create anonymity.
