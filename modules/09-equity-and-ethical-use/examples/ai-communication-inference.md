# Synthetic Example: AI Inference from Employee Communications

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A leader proposes using email, meeting, calendar, and chat metadata to estimate
M/S/E automatically.

The proposed model would count executive contact, response time, sentiment, and
network centrality.

## Ethical-use gate

```text
Purpose: personnel scoring
Evidence relevance: weak and context-poor
Collection: covert digital exhaust
Protected proxy risk: high
Employment-decision risk: high
Contestability: low
Disposition: prohibit
```

The organization may instead examine disclosed workflow bottlenecks or
aggregate forum design without inferring individual advocacy, commitment, or
potential.
