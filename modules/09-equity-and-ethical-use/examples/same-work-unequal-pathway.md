# Synthetic Example: Same Work, Unequal Pathway

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

Two people contribute comparable work. One attends a recurring senior forum;
the other works from a customer site and is not included.

## Evidence

```text
Person A S: 7-8
Evidence: next-level leader names and backs the contribution

Person B S: 1-2
Evidence: next-level leader has not received specific work evidence
Access condition: customer-site role is excluded from the forum
```

## Interpretation

The scores may accurately describe different advocacy evidence.

They do not establish different capability or effort. The organization reviews
the exposure and attribution pathway rather than adding demographic or
work-location correction points to Person B's score.
