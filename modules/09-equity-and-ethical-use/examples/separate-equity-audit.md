# Synthetic Example: Separate Aggregate Equity Audit

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

An organization wants to know whether visible stretch assignments are
distributed fairly.

## Development lane

Managers keep job-relevant assignment records:

```text
eligibility criteria
people informed
people considered
selection rationale
support provided
completion and attribution
```

No protected characteristics appear in individual LIFT records.

## Equity-audit lane

A qualified analytics and privacy team uses separately governed demographic
data to examine aggregate selection and support patterns.

The team:

- defines the question and authority;
- limits access;
- checks small and intersectional groups for re-identification;
- reports uncertainty;
- does not publish individual scores;
- does not change individual M/S/E arithmetically;
- recommends changes to criteria, nomination, support, and review.

The lanes answer related questions without becoming one personnel dossier.
