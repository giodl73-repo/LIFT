## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the ethical-use gate;
- counterexamples where an equity review increased surveillance or
  retaliation;
- privacy-safe pathway reviews in small organizations;
- safe aggregation across intersectional groups;
- retention and deletion practices across jurisdictions;
- accessible alternatives to score-based development conversations;
- evidence that people can contest records without career harm;
- examples from shift, field, contract, union, public-sector, and nonprofit
  work;
- governance for approved private AI tools;
- ways to detect secondary use after a record is shared.
