# Module 09 Learning

## Source lesson

The private source correctly recognized that proximity, networks, career shape,
and ambiguous evidence can produce unequal visibility and advocacy.

It then encoded those concerns as generated subgroup accuracy, identity and
work-mode penalties, demographic inference, score corrections, and hiring
rules. That approach turned evidence of structural inequality into a more
complex personnel classifier.

## Changes in the public rewrite

- subgroup score correction became process and opportunity review;
- hiring inference was prohibited rather than recalibrated;
- advocacy observation was separated from capability judgment;
- safety-before-score became the governing rule;
- Purpose-Limit-Inspect-Protect-Contest-Expire became the data lifecycle;
- development and aggregate equity audit became separate governed lanes;
- protected data was excluded from individual LIFT records;
- consent gained a workplace-power test;
- records gained purpose, audience, correction, retention, and deletion fields;
- anti-retaliation became a required condition;
- small-group aggregation gained re-identification limits;
- digital exhaust and workplace surveillance were prohibited as evidence;
- AI use was limited to disclosed formatting and deidentified assistance;
- employment decisions, talent rankings, and shadow systems were prohibited;
- misuse gained a stop, restrict, correct, delete, and route response;
- all remote, demographic, discipline, career-path, and subgroup arithmetic
  was removed.

## Open learning questions

- Is the ethical-use gate clear enough to stop a manager-led score rollout?
- Can pathway distributions be reviewed without creating a shadow system?
- Can protected demographic analysis remain separate in practice?
- Which record fields are still excessive?
- How should a person safely contest evidence under a powerful manager?
- Can small organizations protect confidentiality?
- What retention guidance is useful without pretending laws are universal?
- Which AI assistance remains safe after real workplace context is added?
