# Synthetic Example: A Resilient Manager Transition

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A manager will leave the organization in six weeks. Several resource
commitments, cross-team decisions, and development opportunities were discussed
through that manager.

## Continuity work

```text
Context: decision notes identify rationale, uncertainty, and owners.
Evidence: contributors and peers can verify major work directly.
Resources: requests and decision status are recorded.
Opportunity: nominations identify criteria and current commitment, not a
guaranteed outcome.
Relationships: relevant working connections become direct or shared.
```

The incoming manager reviews the evidence rather than inheriting every judgment
as fact.

## Interpretation

Some trust and sponsorship may not transfer. Evidence and legitimate working
relationships need not reset to zero.

The transition exposes dependencies without pretending to predict a numerical
career effect.
