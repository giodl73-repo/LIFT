## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the five-channel pathway map;
- evidence about which forms of redundancy improve continuity;
- counterexamples where direct access harms coordination or safety;
- low-burden ways to inspect unequal access;
- pathway patterns in distributed, frontline, shift, and non-office work;
- evidence separating useful boundary work from overload;
- examples where strong organizational mechanisms reduce dependence on a
  manager's personal network.
