# Module 06 Learning

## Source lesson

The private source made an important distinction between evidence position and
evidence movement. It also recognized that lack of awareness, structural
friction, counterevidence, and active blocking should not receive the same
response.

It weakened those insights with ordinal score subtraction, a momentum equation,
fixed state thresholds, resistance and friction coefficients, generated
promotion forecasts, dramatic motive inference, and universal exit advice.

## Changes in the public rewrite

- momentum became sustained evidence improvement rather than physical force;
- movement became dimension-specific states rather than one aggregate arrow;
- ordinal scores were not subtracted or summed;
- holding was separated from stalled through a defined evidence experiment;
- friction, counterevidence, resistance, and uncertainty gained distinct
  definitions;
- resistance became observed behavior or process rather than a motive claim;
- a bounded experiment replaced unlimited investment;
- stop conditions became explicit;
- resets preserved durable evidence while re-evaluating relationship-specific
  evidence;
- fixed remote, manager, and organizational coefficients were removed;
- formal safety and correction routes replaced gossip and hidden-campaign
  diagnosis;
- every promotion, recovery, attrition, and exit prediction was removed.

## Open learning questions

- Are six movement states too many for ordinary use?
- Can practitioners distinguish holding from stalled reliably?
- Does the resistance record prevent motive inflation?
- Which review triggers work outside periodic office settings?
- How should mixed movement across M/S/E be summarized?
- Can bounded experiments reduce wasted effort without encouraging premature
  exit?
- What evidence should survive each kind of reset?
