# Synthetic Example: Resistance Versus Counterevidence

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Counterevidence

A person asks to lead a high-risk project. The decision-maker declines because
the person has not yet led the required operational review. The same criterion
was published in advance and applied to other candidates.

```text
Condition: counterevidence or incomplete evidence
Response: gain or demonstrate the missing capability
```

## Resistance

The person completes the agreed operational review successfully. At the next
decision, the owner says that requirement no longer matters and introduces an
unpublished criterion not applied to another candidate.

When asked for the current criteria, the owner refuses to provide them.

```text
Condition: observed blocking pattern
Motive: unknown
Response: document the criteria change and use an available correction or
review route
```

An unfavorable decision is not automatically resistance. Inconsistent
treatment of relevant evidence can be.
