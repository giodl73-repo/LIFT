# Synthetic Example: A Reorganization Reset

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A person's direct manager, next-level leader, and organizational mission all
change during a reorganization.

Before the change:

- contribution evidence was well documented;
- the former manager advocated repeatedly;
- the former next-level leader understood the work;
- no senior sponsorship act had been observed.

## Reset review

```text
Durable contribution record: carry forward with context
Former manager advocacy: historical evidence, no longer the current M pathway
Former next-level awareness: historical, relevance depends on current role
Current M: unknown while the new manager learns the work
Current S: unknown
E: remains unknown
```

The prior work did not disappear. Relationship-specific evidence must be
re-established or replaced.

Calling every dimension weaker would overstate what is known.
