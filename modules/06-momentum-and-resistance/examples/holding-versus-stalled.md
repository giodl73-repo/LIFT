# Synthetic Example: Holding Versus Stalled

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Holding

A senior sponsor supported a specific scope decision two months ago. No similar
decision or relevant forum has occurred since.

The evidence is recent and still applicable. No change was expected.

```text
E movement state: holding
```

## Stalled

A manager and contributor agree that the next evidence condition is direct
presentation of results in the next portfolio review. The review occurs, the
work is relevant, but the contribution is again represented only through a
generic team summary.

The manager cannot explain why the agreed route was not used.

```text
S movement state: stalled
Confidence: medium
Next decision: clarify ownership of the next presentation and whether direct
access is actually available
```

Elapsed time alone did not distinguish the states. A real opportunity to test
the pathway did.
