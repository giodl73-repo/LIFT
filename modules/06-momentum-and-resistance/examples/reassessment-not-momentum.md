# Synthetic Example: Reassessment Is Not Momentum

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A person initially records S as `4` based mostly on inference. After reviewing
the rubric, they locate two recent reported advocacy acts and revise S to `6`.

No new organizational event occurred between the two assessments.

## Interpretation

```text
Score changed: yes
Evidence accuracy improved: yes
Pathway movement occurred: unknown
Momentum established: no
```

The better assessment matters. It should not be described as velocity or
organizational movement.

Future movement requires evidence that the next-level pathway changes over
time.
