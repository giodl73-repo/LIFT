## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the movement states and records;
- counterexamples where apparent strengthening was temporary visibility;
- evidence about appropriate review triggers in different work types;
- safe practices for identifying observed blocking behavior;
- examples of legitimate criteria changes that should not be called resistance;
- reset handling across manager changes, leave, reorganization, and profession
  change;
- low-burden ways to preserve longitudinal evidence;
- evidence about when route changes outperform continued investment.
