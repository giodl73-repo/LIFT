# Module 01 Public Sources

These sources support adjacent concepts. They do not validate the LIFT scale,
M/S/E thresholds, or universal score corrections.

## Self-assessment limits

- Davis, D. A., et al. (2006), "Accuracy of Physician Self-assessment Compared
  With Observed Measures of Competence: A Systematic Review,"
  *JAMA*. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16954489/).

  This is a profession-specific review. It supports caution about unaided
  self-assessment, not a claim that workplace advocacy follows the same error
  pattern.

## Feedback seeking

- Bălăceanu, A., et al. (2021), "Feedback-Seeking Behavior in Organizations: A
  Meta-Analysis and Systematical Review of Longitudinal Studies,"
  *The Spanish Journal of Psychology*.
  [Cambridge record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/spanish-journal-of-psychology/article/abs/feedbackseeking-behavior-in-organizations-a-metaanalysis-and-systematical-review-of-longitudinal-studies/6FFDC8E5984077B6AD8C5BF8292A0A63).

- Crans, S., et al. (2022), "Learning leadership and feedback seeking
  behavior," *Frontiers in Psychology*.
  [Full text](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.890861/full).

- Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development, "Performance feedback: an
  evidence review."
  [Evidence review](https://www.cipd.org/globalassets/media/knowledge/knowledge-hub/evidence-reviews/performance-feedback-evidence-review_tcm18-111378.pdf).

These sources support feedback seeking as a contextual workplace-learning
behavior. They do not imply that all feedback is accurate, safe, actionable, or
free from power dynamics.

## Mentorship and sponsorship

- Ibarra, H. (2022), "What We Still Misunderstand About Mentorship and
  Sponsorship," *Harvard Business Review IdeaCast*.
  [Transcript](https://hbr.org/podcast/2022/11/what-we-still-misunderstand-about-mentorship-and-sponsorship).

This source distinguishes developmental support from sponsorship that uses
organizational and political capital. LIFT adapts that distinction without
claiming a universal score boundary between mentor and sponsor.

## Psychological safety

- American Psychological Association, "Psychological Safety in the Changing
  Workplace," 2024 Work in America research.
  [Report](https://www.apa.org/pubs/reports/work-in-america/2024/psychological-safety).

This source informs the safety boundary around direct feedback seeking. It does
not establish that any specific LIFT question is safe in a given relationship.
