# Module 01 Learning

## Source lesson

The private source correctly identified an observation gap: relationship warmth
and visible support do not reveal everything that happens in a person's
absence.

It then overreached by assigning exact population corrections, interpreting
ambiguity as hidden opposition, and treating generated studies as empirical
validation.

## Changes in the public rewrite

- fixed score corrections became evidence classes and confidence;
- automatic upward correction of E became `unknown`;
- the sponsor question became one evidence-gathering practice;
- vague answers became weak or unknown evidence rather than a predetermined
  score;
- defensive answers became a conversation-safety signal, not proof of motive;
- triangulation became legitimate corroboration rather than private
  surveillance;
- a safety gate was added before direct feedback seeking;
- mentorship, visibility, and sponsorship were separated without devaluing
  mentorship;
- repeated scoring was limited to meaningful evidence changes;
- public research was used only for adjacent claims and limitations.

## Open learning questions

- Can practitioners classify evidence consistently?
- Does the sponsor question improve clarity without damaging trust?
- When should an account from a manager be treated as reported rather than
  direct evidence?
- Do score ranges reduce false precision or create confusion?
- Can a low-safety alternative produce useful evidence without encouraging
  gossip?
- How should stale evidence change confidence before it changes the score?
