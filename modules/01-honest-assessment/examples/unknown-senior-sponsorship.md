# Synthetic Example: Unknown Senior Sponsorship

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Signals

- A senior leader referenced the initiative in a broad meeting.
- The practitioner has not participated in a senior decision about the work.
- Their manager believes the initiative is receiving positive attention.
- No one can identify a decision, resource, protection, or scope change tied to
  informed sponsorship.

## Assessment

```text
E = unknown
Confidence = low
```

The broad reference is ambient evidence of visibility. The manager's statement
is reported evidence that attention may exist. Neither supports an automatic
upward or downward score correction.

## Next evidence

Useful evidence could include:

- a senior decision that protects or expands the initiative;
- a request for the practitioner or team to shape broader direction;
- specific context relayed through a legitimate organizational channel.

Until then, `unknown` is more honest than either `3` or `7`.
