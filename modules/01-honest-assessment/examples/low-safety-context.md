# Synthetic Example: Low-Safety Context

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A practitioner receives inconsistent feedback. Their manager has reacted
poorly to prior questions, controls near-term work allocation, and has punished
disagreement publicly.

## Unsafe response

LIFT does not require the practitioner to ask a direct sponsor question or
confront the manager about hidden advocacy.

## Safer assessment

```text
M = unknown
Confidence = low
```

Record:

- documented changes in scope and expectations;
- specific feedback already provided;
- decisions affecting opportunities or credit;
- contradictions that need clarification;
- available formal support channels.

If appropriate, seek help from a trusted advisor or established employee
support process. Do not ask colleagues to monitor private conversations.

The immediate problem may be management safety rather than score accuracy.
