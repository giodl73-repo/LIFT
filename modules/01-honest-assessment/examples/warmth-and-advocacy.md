# Synthetic Example: Warmth and Advocacy

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Initial judgment

```text
M = 9
Reason: My manager is supportive, gives thoughtful feedback, and says I am
doing excellent work.
```

## Evidence classification

| Evidence | Class | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Thoughtful feedback in weekly 1:1s | direct | strong mentorship and management support |
| Encouraging statements about future growth | direct | positive intent, but not an advocacy act |
| Broader scope assigned with explicit ownership | direct | some support and trust |
| No known example of representation in another decision forum | unknown | advocacy in absence is untested |

## Revised assessment

```text
M = 6–7
Confidence = low
```

The lower range does not mean the manager is ineffective or unsupportive. It
means the available evidence demonstrates mentorship more clearly than
advocacy.

## Safe next question

> Can you share a recent example of how you represented my work or growth in a
> conversation I was not part of?

A specific answer could increase confidence. A vague answer would leave the
range provisional rather than proving opposition.
