## Open evidence needs

- independent use of every template;
- fields that users consistently skip;
- records that create anxiety or excessive ceremony;
- counterexamples where a shared conversation record increases risk;
- storage practices across different environments;
- whether event-based review is understandable;
- whether users preserve evidence with score history;
- deletion and closure behavior;
- accessibility of plain Markdown;
- unmet needs that remain after template simplification.
