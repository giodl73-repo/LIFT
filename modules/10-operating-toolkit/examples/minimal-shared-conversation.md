# Synthetic Example: A Minimal Shared Conversation

This example is invented to demonstrate the toolkit.

## Private file

The person has a longer private profile containing uncertainty and personal
decision criteria.

They do not share it.

## Shared record

```text
Permitted purpose: development conversation about contribution attribution
Participants: person and manager
What I am sharing: two observable attribution examples
What I am not sharing: private career options and unverified peer comments

Question: how will shared contribution be represented in the next review?
Specific evidence: named in the architecture note; omitted from the executive
summary
Counterevidence or uncertainty: summary may have been shortened for space
Requested next action: use explicit shared attribution in the next update

Agreed commitment: manager will review the attribution line with both owners
Review trigger: next executive update
Who may retain this record: both participants
Deletion or archive condition: after the review unless needed to preserve the
commitment
```

The shared record contains only what the conversation needs.
