# Synthetic Example: Refusing Centralized Team Scores

This example is invented to demonstrate the toolkit.

## Request

A leader asks every manager to submit employee M/S/E scores to a shared
spreadsheet so leadership can identify weak talent and promotion readiness.

## Ethical-use check

```text
Proposed purpose: employee ranking and promotion screening
Permitted under ETHICAL_USE.md: no
Necessary fields: none
Person can review or safely contest: no
Employment-decision use excluded: no

Disposition: discard
Reason: centralized individual scoring, comparison, and employment-decision use
are prohibited
Correction route: stop collection and remove existing copies
Protected concern route: qualified organizational process
```

The toolkit does not offer a safer dashboard design.

If leadership wants to inspect unequal access to opportunity, it must define a
separately governed aggregate equity question that does not expose individual
LIFT profiles or become a substitute talent ranking.
