# Synthetic Example: A Team Pathway Review Without Ranking

This example is invented to demonstrate the toolkit.

## Situation

A manager notices that several contributors learn about cross-team
opportunities after decisions are nearly complete.

The manager does not collect individual LIFT scores.

## Pathway record

```text
Purpose: inspect whether opportunity information reaches the team fairly
Dimension or channel: opportunity
Current route: senior planning forum -> manager chat -> ad hoc invitations
Observed failure: invitations depend on who happens to be present
Who has and lacks legitimate access: the manager has forum access; most
contributors do not

Bounded action: publish eligible opportunity criteria and an opt-in notice
after the next planning forum
Owner: manager
Support required: forum permission to share the criteria
Expected evidence: eligible contributors can see and respond to the same notice
Stop condition: opportunity contains confidential staffing information
Review trigger: next comparable opportunity
```

The review examines a pathway. It does not compare employees, average profiles,
or classify talent.
