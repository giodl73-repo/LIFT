```text
Purpose: understand whether my current manager advocates for the scope I own
Assessment date: 2026-08-07
Evidence window: recent two consequential decisions
Review trigger: next resource decision

M
Provisional score or range: 6-7
Observable evidence: manager accurately presented the work in one planning
review and asked for resources
Counterevidence: attribution was omitted in a later cross-team update
Unknowns: no evidence under meaningful disagreement
Confidence: medium
What would change the score: repeated accurate representation when resources
```

question.
