# Synthetic Example: Closing and Deleting a Record

This example is invented to demonstrate the toolkit.

## Situation

A person created an environment-diligence record for an offer. They declined
the offer, and no commitment or legal need remains.

## Closure

```text
Purpose ended: decision completed
Historical value: low
Shared access: none
Action: delete the filled record
Retain: one non-identifying learning note about questions to ask in future
Reason: detailed employer and interview evidence is no longer necessary
```

The person does not retain the record merely because it might be interesting
later.
