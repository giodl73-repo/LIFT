# Synthetic Example: A Transition Snapshot and Archive

This example is invented to demonstrate the toolkit.

## Situation

A person's reporting line changes during a reorganization.

## Snapshot

```text
Transition: new direct manager and changed senior-review forum
Effective date: 2026-08-07
Work-relevant change: direct work remains; advocacy and resource authority move

M evidence status: stale
S evidence status: paused
E evidence status: continuing

Evidence carried: delivered scope and public decision record
Evidence not portable: former manager's private advocacy
Commitments requiring disposition: staffing request
Material unknowns: new manager's authority in the resource forum

New operating contract: review scope, decision rights, and the staffing request
Support owner: new manager
Review triggers: first scope review and staffing disposition
```

The person archives the pre-transition profile as a dated historical snapshot.
They create a separate current profile and leave unsupported dimensions
`unknown` rather than overwriting history or assigning a reset score.
