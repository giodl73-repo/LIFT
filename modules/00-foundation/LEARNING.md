# Module 00 Learning

## Initial source lesson

The private source mixed three valuable ideas with excessive certainty:

1. organizational advocacy occurs at several layers;
2. observable acts are more useful than vague reassurance;
3. the weakest evidence pathway deserves attention.

The same source attached universal thresholds, precise predictions, and
simulation-derived statistics to those ideas. The public rewrite preserves the
model while removing claims that cannot currently be defended.

## Changes in the public rewrite

- `Skip` became `next-level or skip` so the model works outside one org shape.
- `Executive` became `executive or senior sponsor` for smaller and flatter
  organizations.
- fixed gates became an ordinal evidence rubric;
- unknown became an explicit state rather than a score of 5;
- weakest-link language became diagnostic rather than deterministic;
- score records gained counterevidence, confidence, date, and change criteria;
- promotion prediction moved outside the module's claims;
- equity, structural access, and misuse limits became first-class.

## Open learning questions

- Do practitioners distinguish advocacy from visibility?
- Can people use `unknown` without feeling forced to invent a score?
- Are the score bands specific enough to reproduce?
- Does the model remain useful where one person occupies S and E?
- Does the framework increase agency or merely increase career anxiety?
