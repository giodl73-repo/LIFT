## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the six environment lenses;
- examples from frontline, shift, contract, public-sector, and nonprofit work;
- minimum-diligence practices for candidates with limited leverage;
- evidence about reverse references and candidate reactions;
- safe ways to discuss known reorganizations and role uncertainty;
- environment evidence briefs that remain honest after conditions change;
- counterexamples where consistent interview answers concealed poor conditions;
- accessibility and privacy requirements for reciprocal diligence.
