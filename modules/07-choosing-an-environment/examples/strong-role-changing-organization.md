# Synthetic Example: Strong Role, Changing Organization

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A role has attractive scope, fair compensation, and a manager who gives
specific examples of delegation and development.

The organization has announced a restructuring, and the future reporting line
is undecided.

## Decision record

```text
Must be true: role retains meaningful decision authority
Strong evidence: current scope and manager practice
Material unknown: future manager and team ownership
Consequence if wrong: role could become coordination without authority
Reversibility: moderate
Mitigation: written current scope, named owner for post-change decisions,
conversation with likely next-level leader
Review trigger: reporting-line decision
```

## Interpretation

The role is not automatically bad because change is underway. The candidate
does not pretend the current manager evidence predicts the post-change
environment.
