# Synthetic Example: Minimum Diligence Under Limited Leverage

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A candidate needs income quickly, has no competing offer, and cannot request
several additional interviews.

## Minimum diligence

The candidate confirms:

- actual manager;
- core duties and decision authority;
- work location and schedule;
- on-call and travel;
- compensation and offer conditions;
- why the role is open;
- known reorganization;
- how performance is understood.

The organization cannot arrange a peer conversation before the decision
deadline.

## Record

```text
Team conditions: unknown
Peer account: unavailable
Core role terms: written
Manager practice: one specific delegation example
Risk tolerance: candidate accepts the unknown because immediate income matters
Review trigger: first material mismatch after joining
```

The candidate made a constrained decision, not a careless one.
