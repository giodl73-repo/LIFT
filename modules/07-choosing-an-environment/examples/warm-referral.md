# Synthetic Example: Warm Referral Without Guaranteed Sponsorship

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A respected senior contact introduces a candidate to a team and strongly
encourages the candidate to join.

The hiring manager did not initiate the introduction.

## Diligence

The candidate clarifies:

- whether the manager supports the role and proposed scope;
- who owns performance and work decisions;
- what the senior contact actually committed to;
- whether the contact will remain involved;
- how ordinary team members gain context and opportunity.

## Interpretation

The referral provides access and useful context.

It does not establish future M, S, or E. The candidate does not assume that
senior attention will continue or that the manager has accepted an external
sponsorship arrangement.
