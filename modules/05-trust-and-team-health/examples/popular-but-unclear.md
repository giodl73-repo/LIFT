# Synthetic Example: Popular but Unclear

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A manager is warm, available, and well liked. Team members say the manager
cares about them.

Priorities change frequently, decision ownership is unclear, and work is often
redone.

## Three records

```text
Management acts:
- regular one-to-ones
- fast personal support
- no durable priority or decision record

Team experience:
- high interpersonal warmth
- reluctance to make decisions without confirmation
- frustration about rework

Operating context and outcomes:
- several stakeholder changes
- repeated decision delay
- delivery remains inconsistent
```

## Interpretation

Warmth and care are real. They do not establish direction or decision clarity.

The next action is not to reduce care. It is to make priorities, ownership, and
tradeoffs inspectable.
