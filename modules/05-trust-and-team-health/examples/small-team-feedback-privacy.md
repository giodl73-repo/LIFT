# Synthetic Example: Small-Team Feedback Privacy

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A team has three people in visibly different roles. A pulse survey asks for
role, tenure, location, ratings, and written comments.

Although names are removed, the manager could identify every respondent.

## Unsafe design

- publish role-level results;
- quote comments verbatim;
- promise anonymity;
- ask the manager to infer who needs coaching;
- retain raw responses indefinitely.

## Safer response

The organization does not issue a team-level anonymous report.

Instead, a qualified confidential reviewer:

- explains the privacy boundary;
- gathers work-condition evidence;
- reports only sufficiently broad themes;
- provides a separate protected route for serious concerns;
- limits raw-data access and retention;
- tells participants what action can and cannot be reported back.

Aggregation is not privacy when the group is identifiable.
