# Synthetic Example: Upward Reputation, Team Concerns

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A manager has strong senior sponsorship and a reputation for dependable
delivery.

Several team members confidentially describe public blame, unpredictable
standards, and fear of raising delivery risk.

## Unsafe responses

```text
The sponsor says the manager is excellent, so the concerns are mistaken.
Several people complained, so the manager is guilty of every interpretation.
Tell the manager who complained so they can resolve it directly.
```

## Safe initial response

1. preserve the confidential reports;
2. separate observed behavior from inference;
3. assess severity and retaliation risk;
4. look for recurring settings and affected groups;
5. use an independent formal route if conduct may be serious;
6. give the manager behaviorally specific information when safe;
7. do not allow sponsorship to pre-decide the result.

Strong upward support and recurring team concerns create a contradiction to
investigate, not numbers to average.
