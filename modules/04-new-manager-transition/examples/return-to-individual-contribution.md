# Synthetic Example: Returning to Individual Contribution

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

After two management cycles, a person concludes that they prefer deep
specialist work and do not want responsibility for performance and employment
decisions.

They have established clear team routines and supported a successor.

## Transition

The organization:

- treats the decision as a role-fit choice rather than failure;
- preserves recognition for valid management work;
- creates a documented handoff;
- agrees on boundaries so the former manager does not undermine the successor;
- provides a meaningful specialist role rather than a symbolic demotion.

## Interpretation

Management experience can create useful learning without becoming a permanent
identity.

A respected return path makes the original choice to try management safer and
more honest.
