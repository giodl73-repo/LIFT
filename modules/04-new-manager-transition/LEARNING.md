# Module 04 Learning

## Source lesson

The private source correctly recognized that individual-contribution evidence
does not automatically transfer into management evidence. It also named a
common refuge: continuing to perform familiar specialist work while team
clarity, development, feedback, and delegation remain unattended.

It weakened those observations with a fixed score reset, a universal 90-day
clock, generated correlations, weighted behaviors, promotion effects, and a
diagnostic identity label.

## Changes in the public rewrite

- the score reset became an evidence transition;
- the fixed clock became four repeatable passes;
- the private identity label became the non-diagnostic output refuge;
- management work became Frame, Enable, Observe, Develop, and Represent;
- direct contribution gained player-coach and emergency guardrails;
- delegation became an explicit contract rather than task transfer;
- team experience remained evidence without becoming a hidden score;
- inherited and structural conditions were separated from management acts;
- organizations and the manager's manager gained explicit responsibilities;
- management stopped being treated as mandatory advancement;
- returning to individual contribution became a valid outcome;
- all behavioral weights, thresholds, timelines, and promotion claims were
  removed.

## Open learning questions

- Do the four passes help a new manager choose useful work?
- Is "output refuge" recognizable without becoming stigmatizing?
- Can the evidence record separate contribution from inherited conditions?
- Are the direct-contribution guardrails realistic for player-coach roles?
- How should a manager gather team experience safely?
- What support should be mandatory before assigning people-management duties?
- Does the module make leaving management feel legitimately available?
