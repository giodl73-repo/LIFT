## Open evidence needs

- independent use of the four transition passes;
- examples from non-technical, frontline, shift, and volunteer management;
- evidence about player-coach role design;
- evidence separating productive direct work from the output refuge;
- transition support practices that reduce harm to teams;
- ways to gather team experience without surveillance or retaliation;
- examples of respected transitions back to individual-contribution roles;
- counterexamples where delegation reduces rather than improves outcomes.
