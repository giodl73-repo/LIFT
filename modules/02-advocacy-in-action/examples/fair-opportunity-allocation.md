# Synthetic Example: Fair Opportunity Allocation

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A manager has one cross-organization project and six qualified team members.
The same two people received the previous visible assignments.

## Review

```text
Capability needed: coordination under ambiguity
Development value: broader decision experience
Selection criteria: relevant evidence, readiness, interest, available capacity
Prior distribution: two people received most visible work
Support: weekly sponsor check-in and named technical advisors
Consent: candidate can decline without penalty
```

## Decision

The manager selects a qualified person who has not previously received similar
access, explains the criteria privately to candidates, and creates smaller
decision roles for two others.

The decision is not fair merely because a different person was selected.
Fairness comes from relevant criteria, meaningful support, transparent process,
and attention to repeated distribution.
