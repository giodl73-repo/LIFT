# Synthetic Example: Honest Advocacy After a Miss

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A project lead predicted a substantial improvement. The delivered change
produced only a small gain, but the experiment identified a hidden dependency
that changes the remaining plan.

## Distorted stories

Overprotection:

> The project was a success and the original prediction was basically right.

Scapegoating:

> The lead failed to deliver the promised result.

## Honest advocacy

> The predicted improvement did not occur. The lead owns the estimate and
> completed the agreed measurement. Their experiment identified a dependency
> that invalidates two remaining assumptions and prevented a larger rollout
> mistake. The next assignment should test the dependency with a smaller,
> explicitly bounded prediction.

The manager preserves accountability, valid learning, and future opportunity
without converting a miss into either success theater or permanent stigma.
