# Synthetic Example: Credit Without Inflation

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A cross-team reliability improvement succeeds. One engineer designed the
recovery mechanism, another found the triggering condition, an operations
specialist built the rollout plan, and the broader team executed the change.

## Weak advocacy

> Alex led the reliability transformation and delivered the entire result.

This creates a simple hero story by erasing shared contribution.

## Accurate advocacy

> Alex designed the recovery mechanism and coordinated the technical decision.
> Sam identified the triggering condition, Priya built the staged rollout, and
> the service teams executed and validated the change. Together they reduced
> recovery time while preserving rollback safety.

## LIFT sequence

```text
See: distinguish design, diagnosis, rollout, and execution.
Name: connect each contribution to the correct person.
Connect: explain the operational result and safety constraint.
Open: invite the relevant contributors to the follow-up review.
Back: preserve attribution if the story is later compressed.
Transfer: let contributors explain their own decisions directly.
```

Accurate shared credit is not weaker advocacy. It is more trustworthy.
