# Synthetic Example: Sponsorship With Consent

This example is invented to demonstrate the method.

## Situation

A senior leader wants someone to present a successful project at a public
industry event. The manager believes the project lead would benefit from the
visibility.

## Consent conversation

The manager explains:

- the audience and public nature of the event;
- preparation and travel expectations;
- why the lead is being considered;
- available support;
- alternatives, including a co-presenter or written case study;
- that declining will not be treated as lack of ambition.

The lead chooses a co-presentation because of current personal constraints.

## Interpretation

The manager still advocates:

- credit remains attached to the lead;
- the opportunity is adapted rather than withdrawn;
- private circumstances are not shared as part of the story;
- declining one format does not become a career signal.
