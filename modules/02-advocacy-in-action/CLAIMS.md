## Open evidence needs

- practitioner testing of the six-action sequence;
- evidence on consent and unwanted visibility;
- fair-opportunity practices across different job types;
- counterexamples where direct access undermines rather than improves work;
- evidence about manager sponsorship capacity without creating a universal
  threshold;
- measures that reveal unequal opportunity without creating employee
  surveillance.
