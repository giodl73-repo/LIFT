# LIFT Cross-Cutting Practices

Modules define LIFT. Practices compose several modules around a recurring
problem.

They do not add:

- a new score;
- a new advocacy dimension;
- an employment-decision rule;
- a required workflow;
- evidence that the underlying practice improves organizational outcomes.

## Choose a practice

| If you need to... | Use |
|---|---|
| reduce repeated self-documentation, visibility labor, or proof burden | [Reduce evidence burden](evidence-burden.md) |
| represent shared, enabling, maintenance, or cross-functional work accurately | [Represent collective contribution](collective-contribution.md) |
| correct a material claim, attribution, context omission, or broken commitment | [Repair and contest a record](repair-and-contestability.md) |
| see the full LIFT journey applied to one situation | [Integrated casebook](casebook.md) |

## Practice contract

Every practice should:

- start from existing module rules;
- name the smallest legitimate purpose;
- distinguish personal action from manager and organization responsibility;
- reuse ordinary work evidence before requesting new documentation;
- preserve uncertainty, counterevidence, and contestability;
- avoid ranking people or allocating employment outcomes;
- name privacy, retaliation, accessibility, and workload limits;
- stop when the purpose ends.

The burden of making work legible should not silently fall on the person with
the least authority or access.

## Portability and AI

These practices require independent testing in:

- frontline and shift work;
- remote and distributed work;
- contingent, contract, and volunteer settings;
- nonprofit and public-sector organizations;
- accessibility-sensitive communication;
- small organizations with limited formal process.

AI may help an authorized person summarize already selected evidence. It must
not mine email, chat, calendars, recordings, or network graphs to infer
contribution, relationships, intent, or M/S/E.

Read [Equity and Ethical Use](../modules/09-equity-and-ethical-use/README.md)
and the [Ethical Use Policy](../ETHICAL_USE.md).
