# Evidence Policy

LIFT separates useful ideas from unsupported certainty.

## Claim labels

Every material claim belongs to one of these classes:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `definition` | Vocabulary or a declared part of the LIFT model |
| `practice` | An observable action or question a practitioner can try |
| `practitioner hypothesis` | A pattern derived from experience but not established as general empirical fact |
| `synthetic model` | An invented scenario, simulation, or formula used to explore a mechanism |
| `externally supported` | A claim tied to an independently accessible public source |
| `unknown` | Important but not currently supported well enough to assert |

Definitions are not evidence. Repetition is not validation. A simulated panel
is not an external expert review.

## Numerical claims

A public numerical claim must provide:

- the population and context;
- the measurement method;
- the source;
- uncertainty or limitations;
- a reason the number applies to the current use.

Otherwise, remove the number or label it as a synthetic example.

## Scoring evidence

An M/S/E score records a current judgment, not a fact about another person's
private thoughts. Every score should include:

```text
Score or score range:
Observable evidence:
Counterevidence:
Evidence date:
Confidence:
What would change the score:
```

Do not infer advocacy from warmth, title, meeting attendance, or generic praise
alone. If the evidence does not distinguish adjacent values, record a range
such as `5–6` rather than choosing a more precise number.

## Ethical-use gate

A score is not valid for every purpose merely because its evidence is
documented.

LIFT scores must not be used for hiring, pay, promotion, performance ratings,
discipline, termination, layoff, succession, talent ranking, or retention-risk
decisions.

Before sharing a record, apply the [Ethical Use Policy](ETHICAL_USE.md) and
[Module 09](modules/09-equity-and-ethical-use/README.md). Unsafe collection,
protected information, retaliation risk, unequal evidence opportunity, or
unauthorized secondary use can require restriction, deletion, or routing
outside LIFT.

## Revision

When evidence contradicts a module:

1. preserve the previous claim and reason for change;
2. revise the claim label or wording;
3. update affected examples and guidance;
4. record role findings and dissent;
5. publish the learning.
