# LIFT in Startups and Small Businesses

Use this guide for founder-led, early-stage, family, owner-operated, or other
small organizations where titles, reporting lines, and authority may overlap.

## Conditions to inspect

Inspect:

- who owns, manages, funds, decides, advises, and performs the work;
- where founder, family, board, investor, and functional authority differ;
- changing roles, informal commitments, and undocumented decision rights;
- concentration of customer, technical, operational, and people knowledge;
- whether direct access is safe, consequential, and distributed fairly.

Small does not mean simple, informal does not mean harmless, and founder access
does not automatically establish advocacy.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | decisions, customer outcomes, delivery, operations, cash or resource tradeoffs, recovery | whoever is nearest the founder owns the story |
| context | runway, demand, staffing, role changes, dependencies, inherited commitments | urgency makes every boundary temporary |
| credit | creation, sales, operations, enablement, assurance, maintenance | founder or visible generalist absorbs shared contribution |
| opportunity | ownership, decision access, role scope, customer contact, hiring, leadership | informal trust replaces inspectable criteria |

Prefer small existing records. Do not turn every conversation into governance
or build a permanent personnel archive.

## Interpret M/S/E

One person may hold several layers of authority. Record which functions an act
served, but do not present the overlap as several independent advocacy
pathways.

- **M** is the person's actual direct manager when one exists.
- **S** exists only when a distinct next accountable layer exists.
- **E** may be a founder, owner, board member, or enterprise leader with
  relevant authority and observable sponsorship.

Do not create a distinct score for a layer that does not exist. Explain the
overlap or absence in the evidence record. Where the layer exists but evidence
does not, record `unknown`.

## Synthetic example

An operations lead stabilizes delivery while the founder handles sales and
presents the recovery as a founder-led turnaround.

A bounded response is to correct the operating record, distinguish customer,
resource, and execution decisions, and create one durable pathway that does not
depend on private founder access.

Do not turn founder attention, social closeness, or after-hours availability
into advocacy evidence.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Route ownership, fiduciary, investor, family, safety, wage, discrimination,
harassment, termination, insolvency, and legal disputes to qualified owners.
LIFT does not replace basic management, governance, or employment process.
