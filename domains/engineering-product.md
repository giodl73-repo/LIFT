# LIFT in Engineering and Product Work

Use this guide for software, hardware, data, research, design, product,
technical operations, or other artifact-rich and matrixed work.

## Conditions to inspect

Inspect:

- people-management, technical, product, program, and incident authority;
- shared work across repositories, systems, disciplines, and organizations;
- maintenance, review, quality, accessibility, reliability, and recovery work;
- who presents decisions versus who made or enabled them;
- remote, asynchronous, on-call, and cross-time-zone access.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | designs, decisions, code or hardware changes, experiments, reviews, runbooks, incident actions | activity count replaces contribution context |
| context | constraints, alternatives, rejected options, dependencies, customer or operational evidence | final artifact hides tradeoffs and inherited conditions |
| credit | decision ownership, implementation, enablement, assurance, maintenance, recovery | visible launch or author becomes the whole explanation |
| opportunity | design ownership, technical leadership, customer contact, incident command, roadmap decisions | informal network controls high-context work |

Commits, tickets, documents, reviews, messages, and meetings are sources to
interpret, not productivity or advocacy scores.

## Interpret M/S/E

- **M** remains the direct manager, even when a technical or product lead
  directs part of the work.
- **S** is the next accountable management layer with relevant context and
  opportunity to act.
- **E** is a senior sponsor with enterprise or business authority relevant to
  the work.

Do not merge a matrix of technical, product, and management relationships into
one score. Map the specific evidence route.

## Synthetic example

A reliability engineer coordinates a migration, prevents two known failure
modes, and enables a product launch. The launch summary names only the feature
owner.

A bounded response is to correct the existing decision record using source
artifacts, name implementation, coordination, assurance, and decision
ownership separately, and let the manager carry that evidence into the
consequential forum.

Do not estimate contribution from commit volume or incident-message frequency.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Route security incidents, protected customer information, research integrity,
regulated engineering decisions, formal misconduct, and legal or contractual
disputes to their qualified owners.
