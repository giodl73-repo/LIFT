# LIFT in Consulting, Contract, and Contingent Work

Use this guide for consulting, agency, freelance, temporary, vendor, outsourced,
or other work spanning more than one organization.

## Conditions to inspect

Inspect:

- employing, contracting, client, project, vendor, and professional authority;
- who assigns work, evaluates delivery, controls access, and can change terms;
- contract scope, renewal, rate, staffing, confidentiality, and ownership;
- evidence that can move across organizational boundaries;
- dependence on one client, sponsor, account, or intermediary.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | agreed deliverables, decisions, accepted outcomes, change records, handoffs | client visibility replaces full contribution context |
| context | contract scope, dependencies, client decisions, access, staffing, inherited work | constrained authority is treated as poor ownership |
| credit | delivery, specialist work, coordination, account support, client decision | firm, account lead, or client sponsor receives all credit |
| opportunity | assignment, renewal, extension, reference, scope, direct access | pleasing one intermediary becomes the only pathway |

Use only evidence that the relevant parties are authorized to retain and share.

## Interpret M/S/E

Do not combine separate organizations into one profile.

- An **employing-organization profile** may use the person's actual manager,
  next-level leader, and senior sponsor.
- A **client pathway map** may record client owners and decision routes without
  relabeling them M/S/E.
- A self-employed person without an employing advocacy hierarchy should use the
  pathway map without forcing an M/S/E profile.

## Synthetic example

A contractor resolves a delivery dependency, but the client sees only the
agency lead's final presentation. The contractor is then asked to disclose
client-confidential detail to prove the work internally.

A bounded response is to use the authorized delivery record, have the agency
lead confirm contribution, and preserve confidentiality and contract limits.

Do not demand prohibited evidence or treat client access as a substitute for
internal advocacy.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Route worker classification, contract interpretation, intellectual property,
confidentiality, procurement, payment, retaliation, discrimination, and legal
rights to qualified processes. LIFT does not determine employment status or
contractual entitlement.
