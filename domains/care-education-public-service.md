# LIFT in Care, Education, and Public Service

Use this guide where work serves patients, students, residents, communities,
or the public and professional, statutory, clinical, educational, or public
duties may constrain ordinary management action.

## Conditions to inspect

These settings are not interchangeable. Inspect the actual:

- management, professional, clinical, academic, program, and public authority;
- confidentiality, records, consent, safeguarding, and reporting boundaries;
- case, classroom, service, caseload, funding, and staffing conditions;
- team-based care, instruction, administration, assurance, and continuity work;
- shift, site, language, accessibility, and public-accountability constraints.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | authorized service records, decisions, handoffs, quality improvement, teaching or program artifacts | protected subject records become employee evidence |
| context | acuity, caseload, class or community needs, staffing, funding, duty, inherited conditions | outcome differences are assigned to one worker |
| credit | direct service, coordination, preparation, assurance, supervision, continuity | visible professional or leader erases team contribution |
| opportunity | assignment, rotation, committee, training, research, program or policy work | informal access controls scarce development |

Never copy patient, student, client, beneficiary, or protected public records
into LIFT merely to make work visible.

## Interpret M/S/E

- **M** is the direct manager with relevant employment and advocacy
  responsibility.
- **S** is the next accountable management layer.
- **E** is an enterprise, institutional, system, agency, or senior sponsor when
  that layer is relevant.

Professional or clinical authority may sit outside the management chain. Map it
separately rather than treating it as M, S, or E by default.

A professional, safeguarding, safety, or public duty does not wait for a
favorable M/S/E profile. Follow the qualified duty and reporting route first.

## Synthetic example

A coordinator prevents a service gap by aligning several teams and preserving
continuity. The formal outcome record appropriately centers the person
receiving the service and does not name the coordinator's contribution.

A bounded response is to use an authorized, deidentified operational record of
the coordination decision, preserve the service recipient's privacy, and have
the manager represent the work in the appropriate internal forum.

Do not create a parallel case file or infer impact beyond the available
evidence.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Qualified privacy, safeguarding, clinical, educational, professional,
records-management, union, legal, and public-accountability processes remain
authoritative. LIFT is not legal, clinical, educational, or regulatory advice.
