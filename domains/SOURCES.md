# Domain Field-Guide Source Boundary

The field guides primarily compose existing LIFT definitions, practices, and
ethical boundaries. They make no claim that LIFT has been validated in the
named domains.

The following official sources informed the hard boundary that LIFT must not
replace qualified safety, privacy, records, worker-status, or governance
processes:

| Domain boundary | Primary source |
|---|---|
| worker participation and retaliation protection in safety programs | [US Occupational Safety and Health Administration: Worker Participation](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation) |
| protected health-information privacy | [US Department of Health and Human Services: HIPAA Privacy Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html) |
| student education-record privacy | [US Department of Education: Student Privacy Policy Office](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/) |
| federal public-sector privacy records | [US Office of Personnel Management: Privacy Program](https://www.opm.gov/information-management/privacy-policy/) |
| employee and independent-contractor classification | [US Department of Labor: Misclassification Initiative](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification) |
| nonprofit employee and contractor classification | [US Internal Revenue Service: Independent Contractors versus Employees](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/exempt-organizations-independent-contractors-vs-employees) |
| volunteer status in public agencies | [Electronic Code of Federal Regulations: 29 CFR Part 553 Subpart B](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-V/subchapter-A/part-553/subpart-B) |

These sources are jurisdiction-specific examples, not universal legal rules.
Readers must use the qualified rules and processes that apply to their setting.
