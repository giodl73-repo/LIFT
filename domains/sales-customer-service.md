# LIFT in Sales and Customer Service Work

Use this guide for sales, account management, customer success, support,
service delivery, or other customer-facing work.

## Conditions to inspect

Inspect:

- territory, account, queue, product, pricing, and escalation authority;
- inherited pipeline, demand, account health, service conditions, and coverage;
- presales, implementation, support, operations, partner, and renewal work;
- who owns the customer relationship versus a specific decision;
- schedule, travel, language, accessibility, and emotional-labor demands.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | account decisions, service recovery, documented customer outcomes, handoffs, forecasts with assumptions | revenue or case count becomes the whole contribution story |
| context | territory, demand, product fit, pricing, staffing, inherited risk, customer constraints | unlike conditions are treated as equivalent |
| credit | origination, solution work, delivery, recovery, renewal, enablement | the closer or final contact receives all credit |
| opportunity | account allocation, customer exposure, strategic work, travel, lead distribution | access decisions are hidden behind outcome metrics |

Do not copy confidential customer content into a LIFT record. Use the minimum
authorized description needed to understand the advocacy path.

## Interpret M/S/E

- **M** is the direct manager responsible for understanding and representing
  the person's work.
- **S** is the next accountable sales, service, or functional leader.
- **E** is a senior business sponsor when relevant evidence and advocacy exist.

A customer executive is not automatically E. Customer approval, satisfaction,
or access does not establish internal sponsorship.

## Synthetic example

A support specialist identifies a recurring product defect and coordinates the
evidence that changes a renewal plan. The account summary credits only the
seller who presented the decision.

A bounded response is to correct the shared account record, separate customer
ownership from diagnostic and coordination work, and include the specialist in
the relevant follow-up when useful and wanted.

Do not turn customer sentiment, call recordings, or case volume into an
employee advocacy score.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Route customer complaints, regulated communications, recording consent,
confidential data, compensation disputes, harassment, retaliation, and formal
performance decisions to the appropriate process.
