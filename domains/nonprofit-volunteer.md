# LIFT in Nonprofit and Volunteer Organizations

Use this guide for charities, associations, community groups, foundations,
board-governed organizations, campaigns, or volunteer programs.

## Conditions to inspect

Inspect:

- board, executive, staff, program, funder, partner, and volunteer authority;
- whether the person is staff, volunteer, contractor, member, or officeholder;
- mission, funding, grant, donor, community, and safeguarding constraints;
- unpaid coordination, relationship, maintenance, and continuity work;
- who can safely decline, correct, or leave an assignment.

Mission commitment does not remove power differences or make unlimited labor
reasonable.

## Translate the evidence path

| Channel | Ordinary evidence | Common distortion |
|---|---|---|
| work | program decisions, delivery records, community feedback, grant outputs, governance actions | funder-visible output erases enabling and local work |
| context | restricted funding, volunteer capacity, community need, governance, inherited commitments | resource limits become personal failure |
| credit | design, delivery, facilitation, fundraising, administration, safeguarding, continuity | founder, executive, donor, or public spokesperson becomes the whole story |
| opportunity | board contact, paid roles, grants, leadership, travel, public representation | unpaid access and availability are mistaken for merit |

Do not repurpose donor, beneficiary, member, volunteer, or safeguarding records
as employee-advocacy evidence.

## Interpret M/S/E

- **M** exists only when a person has an actual direct-management
  relationship. Coordination alone does not create M.
- **S** is the next accountable leadership layer when one exists.
- **E** may be an executive, board, owner, or senior sponsor, depending on
  actual authority.

For informal or all-volunteer groups without an advocacy hierarchy, use a
pathway map without forcing an M/S/E profile. Where a layer exists but evidence
does not, record `unknown`.

## Synthetic example

A volunteer coordinator sustains a community program through scheduling,
training, and recovery work. Public recognition names only the executive who
announces the result.

A bounded response is to correct the program record, name paid and unpaid
contributions without ranking them, and change the future reporting pathway.

Do not imply that recognition substitutes for workload limits, expenses,
employment rights, or appropriate role classification.

## Stop or route elsewhere

Route safeguarding, charity governance, donor restrictions, worker
classification, compensation, formal employment, financial, and legal issues
to qualified owners. LIFT does not determine whether a person is legally an
employee, contractor, or volunteer.
